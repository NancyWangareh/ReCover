# ReCover
## Microinsurance Information and Recommendation System
The key is to build something that helps mitumba market traders understand available microinsurance products, compare their options, and choose what suits their needs.
### User interface
#### WhatsApp chatbot 
1. Microinsurance knowledge base
2. Insurance documents, product details, eligibility, premiums, exclusions and claims procedures
#### AI and decision engine
1. Extract information, answer questions, compare products and assess affordability
2. Trader receives guidance
3. Understandable explanations, product comparisons, provider contacts and next steps
